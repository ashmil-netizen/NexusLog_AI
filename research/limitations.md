# Limitations

- Olist is a historical dataset, not a live carrier tracking feed; stage predictions are retrospective simulations.
- Recorded milestone timestamps and item/payment fields may not reflect what a real operator sees at the stated instant.
- Weather, route-level conditions, and operational carrier events are not comprehensively represented.
- Results describe this dataset and may not generalize to another marketplace, country, or time period.
- Delayed and on-time class balance must be read from the generated audit; class imbalance may affect threshold-dependent metrics.
- False-positive and false-negative costs are hypothetical unless supplied from an actual operation.
- Customer/seller identities can recur across chronological partitions, limiting claims about generalization to unseen entities.
- Permutation importance is predictive, not causal.
- The project makes no unsupported claim of algorithmic novelty.
